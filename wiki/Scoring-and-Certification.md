# Scoring and Certification

## Total score

The maximum achievable score is **500 points**, summed across four phases:

| Phase | Maximum | Share of total |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| Phase 1 — Classify | 110 | 22% |
| Phase 2 — Apply | 195 | 39% |
| Phase 3 — Strategize | 110 | 22% |
| Phase 4 — Test Cases | 85 | 17% |
| **Total** | **500** | 100% |

Your **percentage** determines your grade tier. The navbar **Score** updates during play; **Best** stores your highest total in this browser.

---

## Phase 1 scoring detail

| Rule | Points |
|------|--------|
| Correct classification (per card) | +6 |
| All 15 correct (perfect bonus) | +20 |
| Hint used (per card, first time) | −4 |

Phase 1 maximum: (15 × 6) + 20 = **110** (before hint penalties).

---

## Phase 2 scoring detail

Each of the **9 scenarios** awards **20 or 25 points** for a correct answer. There is no partial credit per scenario.

Maximum: **195**.

---

## Phase 3 scoring detail

Each project awards up to **37, 37, or 36 points** depending on how suitable your selected techniques are. The game compares your choices to technique suitability values and caps the score at the project maximum.

Maximum: **110**.

---

## Phase 4 scoring detail

| Rule | Points |
|------|--------|
| Each required test case selected | +6 |
| Out-of-scope item selected | −2 each |

Per-exercise caps: **42** and **43**. Maximum: **85**.

---

## Grade tiers

Grades are based on **percentage of 500**, not raw points alone.

| Grade | Minimum % | Title |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| ISO Master | 90% | Outstanding mastery (≥ 450 pts) |
| Senior Tester | 75% | Strong understanding (≥ 375 pts) |
| Test Engineer | 60% | Good basics (≥ 300 pts) |
| Junior Tester | 40% | Learning in progress (≥ 200 pts) |
| Trainee | 0% | Review recommended |

---

## Certificate

On the results screen, click **Download Certificate** to save a PNG file containing:

- Your player name (from the welcome screen)  
- Grade title and emoji  
- Total points and percentage  
- Per-phase score summary  
- Completion date  
- Course attribution (SENG 436 · Çankaya University)

The certificate is generated client-side using the Canvas API; no data is sent to a server.

---

## Leaderboard

The **Top 10** leaderboard is stored in `localStorage` on your device only. It is **not** shared across browsers or machines and is not suitable for official competitive ranking without a backend.
